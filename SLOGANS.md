# Sloperator Slogans

Ten taglines for the SlopLang interpreter.

1. **Ship it with sparkles.** ✨
   _A nod to `ship` and the mandatory `✨` terminator — the whole language in five words._

2. **`vibe` first, `nah` second.**
   _Control flow for people who make decisions emotionally but need a boolean anyway._

3. **Hallucinate, but deterministically.**
   _`hallucinate` prints; the interpreter doesn't guess. No truthiness, no implicit conversions._

4. **Hold the loadbearing. Ship the slop.**
   _The two binding keywords, and the only thing that ever gets returned._

5. **Ralph it until it's ✅.** 
   _A `ralph` loop runs only while the condition is literally `✅` — no vibes-based truthiness about it._

6. **No truthiness, no mercy.**
   _Conditions must be `✅` or `❌`. There is no implicit conversion, so `vibe 1 {` is a type error, not a lifestyle choice._

7. **Every statement ends in sparkles.**
   _`✨` is mandatory after declarations, assignments, `ship`, and expression statements — the one bit of punctuation the language refuses to be casual about._

8. **Straight quotes are a lexical error.**
   _Strings open with `“` and close with `”`. Curly or nothing — the lexer has opinions and will share them._

9. **`loadbearing` means it.**
   _Assign to a `loadbearing` binding and the interpreter stops you: `cannot assign to loadbearing 'LIMIT'`. Immutability with a load rating._

10. **Em dash in, comment out.**
    _`—` starts a comment that runs to end of line. The only place in SlopLang where you're allowed to ramble._
